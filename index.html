About us

About Dirty D's Mobile RV Detailing

At Dirty D's, we believe your RV deserves a spa day too! Our mobile detailing service brings the shine right to your driveway, using eco-friendly, American-made products that are tough on dirt but gentle on the planet. From pressure washing roofs and cleaning awnings to hand washing, waxing, and oxidation removal, we’ve got your RV covered. Plus, our long-lasting ceramic coatings and rubber roof conditioners ensure your vehicle stays protected and looking fabulous for miles to come. Let us take care of the dirty work while you enjoy the open road!

Answers before you book.

Looking for mobile RV cleaning near you? Here are the details customers ask about most. Or ask our chat assistant in the corner.

Do you come to my RV location?

Yes. Dirty D's is a fully mobile RV detailing service. We come to your driveway, storage lot or campsite in our service area. You can also bring your RV to our location in Wapato.

What does your RV detailing service include?

Services include holding tank cleaning and treatments, interior carpet and upholstery extraction, exterior RV washing, roof and slide-out care, waxing, seal conditioning and protective coatings. See the Services page for every package.

What areas does Dirty D's Mobile RV Detailing serve?

We serve Benton County, Kittitas County, Lewis County and Yakima County in Washington. Share your city, address or campground in the booking form so we can confirm availability.

How much does mobile RV detailing cost?

Exterior and interior packages are priced per linear foot of your RV: $10 per foot for Basic Exterior and Level 1 Interior, $15 for Level 2, and $25 for the Ultimate Exterior and Level 3 Interior packages. Add-ons are priced separately. Your final quote depends on your RV's size, condition and the services you choose, and we confirm it before any work starts.

Can I book an exterior and an interior package together?

Yes. Choose one exterior and one interior package in the booking form and we will confirm the details with you.

Do you offer a ceramic coating?

Yes. The Ultimate Exterior Package includes a premium ceramic coating and wax, a 1.5-year protectant.
